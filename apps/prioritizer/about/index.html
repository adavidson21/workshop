Docket

Decide what to work on next.

A calm, keyboard-first task list that ranks your work for you. Add a task in one line, and Docket tells you what matters today.

Open Docket Android app

Free. No account. Your tasks never leave your device.

What it does

Capture in one line

Type the task with shorthand for who, how valuable, how big and when. Docket parses it as you type.

Ranks it for you

Value, effort and due date become one score. Today, Eisenhower and Value / Effort views all agree. Your own order still wins.

Plans your day

Turn your sprint into time blocks, drop tasks in, and rearrange when the day goes sideways.

Preps your 1:1s

One card per person: what you're waiting on them for, and what to bring up.

Why it exists

Most task apps are great at collecting work and bad at telling you what to do with it. Docket is built for the second part.

  • Less sorting. Triage a pile one task at a time in seconds each.
  • Nothing rots. Stale and overdue work surfaces on its own.
  • Private by design. Saved in your browser or on your phone. No server, no sign up.
  • Works offline. Install it like an app from your browser.
